Model: results/train_x100_stride2400/y_7m_10db_epoch24.pt (x100, trained on windows starting at multiples of 2400)
Data: data/validation/y_7m_10db, windows with stride 4800 starting at a fixed offset

offset 0:    angle 0.68 deg, distance 0.49 m
offset 1200: angle 0.69 deg, distance 1.98 m   -> only the distance gets worse

Labels (mask edges) and signal energy are uniform modulo 2400: no periodicity in the data.
Cause: every recording starts when the walk starts, and the 100 walks of the pool have fixed step times
(FIRST_STEP is random between walks, not between recordings). With windows aligned to the start of the recording
the network learns the arrival time of the steps within the 2400-sample frames and, knowing the walk, the time of
flight, so the distance. 2400 / 16000 s * 343 m/s = 51 m >= max distance in the room, so the phase modulo 2400 is enough.
Not available in a real case (unknown emission time). The classic localizers (TDOA) are not affected.
Fix used: random offset of the training windows (window_stride 4800, offset in [0, 4800) at every epoch).
All the results of the network trained without it (x0.5 ... x30, x100 stride 2400) are affected, also on the test.
